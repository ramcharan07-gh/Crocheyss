the helpful little corner

Questions, answered
with care.

How long does a handmade order take? +

Most ready-to-ship pieces leave our studio in 2–4 working days. Custom pieces usually take 7–14 working days, and we confirm the timeline before starting.

Can I request a different color? +

Absolutely. Use the custom order form or email us with your preferred yarn colors and we will reply with availability and a quote.

How should I care for my crochet piece? +

Keep it dry, spot clean gently with a damp cloth, and let it air dry flat. Avoid bleach, harsh detergents, and high heat.

Do you accept returns? +

Because every piece is handmade, please contact us within 48 hours of delivery if there is a quality issue so we can make it right.
